## Santosh Ayer

I am a graduate researcher in Forest Biology and Management at the
University of Alberta, Canada, supported through a Graduate Research
Assistant Fellowship. My graduate research evaluates how forest
growth-and-yield models represent forest responses to thinning,
including their implications for stand development and forest
management.

My broader research develops transparent and practical approaches for
forest inventory, allometry, biomass estimation, growth modelling, and
carbon accounting, with a particular focus on Nepal.

I want to contribute to Nepal’s forestry sector by developing
accessible, scientifically defensible tools that connect forestry
research with education and operational forest management.

## About this package

Santosh Ayer is the developer and maintainer of `nepalallometry`. The
package implements published models but does not claim authorship of the
underlying equations. Each model, dataset, regulatory document, and
conversion factor is credited to its original source through the package
reference registry.
